TattooBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

TattooBook is designed to help tattoo professionals organize tattoo records, sessions, photos, work profiles, and related information while keeping their data private.

Data Storage

TattooBook stores your tattoo records, client names entered by you, session information, notes, pricing and earnings information, work profiles, preferences, and attached tattoo photos locally on your device. TattooBook does not operate a server or cloud database to receive or store this information.

Photos

TattooBook may request access to photos you choose through the iOS photo picker so that you can attach images to tattoo records. Selected images are copied to the app's private local storage. TattooBook does not upload these photos to its own servers or third-party services.

AI Quick Entry and Assistant Features

The current version of TattooBook processes Quick Entry text and assistant recommendations locally on your device. Information entered into these features is not sent to an external artificial intelligence provider or other server.

Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

TattooBook does not use advertising services, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, attribution services, or third-party crash-reporting services. TattooBook does not use your information to track you across apps or websites.

Data Sharing and Backup Export

TattooBook does not automatically transmit your stored records to the developer or third parties. If you choose to export a backup, TattooBook creates a local backup file and presents the iOS sharing interface. You decide where that file is sent or saved. Tattoo photos are not included in the current backup export.

Accounts

TattooBook does not currently require or provide a user account, login, or registration system.

Data Deletion

Because TattooBook's application data is stored locally, you can remove individual records through the app. Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored application data, subject to Apple's device and backup behavior.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if TattooBook's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

Contact
